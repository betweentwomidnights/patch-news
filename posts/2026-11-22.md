there’s an installable Apple Silicon preview of [gary4local](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer), bringing the mac into the desktop app we’re building for windows and linux. the [signed preview build](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/actions/runs/45045865481) includes an app and disk image, with signature and notarization checks in CI. it’s available as a workflow artifact, rather than a published app release.

the [mac setup](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/blob/31ae77cca576eb2b2fae6c01a7d9a03c781d0f4a/docs/macos-desktop.md) installs the Metal runtimes from inside the app, with model downloads handled separately. automatic app updates aren’t enabled yet. packaging checks don’t establish whether the models make music on real hardware, so generation, training and restart checks on the M4 are the next step.
