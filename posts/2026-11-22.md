there’s an installable Apple Silicon preview of [gary4local](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer). the [signed preview build](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/actions/runs/45045865481) includes the app and disk image, so mac users can try the desktop app we’re building alongside windows and linux.

the [mac work](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/pull/33) also adds Metal runtime setup and stops the local services when you quit. it’s still a preview, and automatic mac app updates aren’t enabled yet.
