there’s an installable Apple Silicon preview of [gary4local](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer), bringing the mac into the desktop app we’re building for windows and linux. the [signed preview build](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/actions/runs/45045865481) includes an app and disk image, with signature and notarization checks in CI. you can find the preview under artifacts on GitHub Actions.

the [mac setup](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/blob/31ae77cca576eb2b2fae6c01a7d9a03c781d0f4a/docs/macos-desktop.md) installs the Metal runtimes from inside the app, with model downloads handled separately. automatic app updates aren’t enabled yet. the next step is making some music on the M4 and checking how the app handles training and restarts.
