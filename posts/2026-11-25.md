you can now run gary’s models on another linux computer and connect from your DAW without an SSH tunnel. [gary4juce v5.0.8](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary4juce/releases/tag/v5.0.8) and [gary4local v0.7.7](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/releases/tag/v0.7.7) pair using an address and token on a trusted local network; the connection isn’t encrypted. on a DGX Spark, we tested pairing and used a test feed to check updates and rollback; the linux desktop app only passed automated checks for this release.

for users extending audio with [yuey](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/yuey.cpp/releases/tag/v0.3.4), updating both apps also adds clearer limits. [gary checks the source before uploading](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary4juce/pull/18) and tells you what to crop, rather than starting an overlong render. when yuey chooses the length, its default 96-second limit now covers the original audio and the new music together.
