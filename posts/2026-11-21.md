[gary4local v0.7.5](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/releases/tag/v0.7.5) adds a linux terminal console for running all seven model services without a desktop app. the x64 and ARM64 downloads include guided model setup, service controls, and SA3 and Carey LoRA training. desktop and terminal sessions share the LoRA library, keeping adapters and training history together.

on the DGX Spark we [started SA3 and Carey on Vulkan and checked that a cancelled SA3 training run kept its checkpoint](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/pull/31). linux x64 still needs hands-on testing, and connecting [gary4juce](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary4juce) from another computer still needs SSH forwarding because the services listen locally. this gives us a way to put the models on a separate linux machine while keeping the DAW on the computer we make music with.
