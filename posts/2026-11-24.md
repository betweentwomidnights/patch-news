[gary4juce v5.0.7](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary4juce/releases/tag/v5.0.7) and [gary4local v0.7.6](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer/releases/tag/v0.7.6) are out, bringing windows, mac and linux onto the same desktop setup. the companion runs models natively on Apple Silicon, replacing the old mac app. we tested fresh startup and generation with six services on an M4 MacBook Air; linux passed CI, and its [plugin preview](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary4juce/releases/tag/v5.0.7-linux-preview.1) adds built-in stem separation with CPU fallback.

the mac companion can now install its own updates, replacing the preview’s manual-update setup. moving from the [old app](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer-mac#this-app-is-retired) means downloading models again and using the migration guide’s converted SA3 LoRA copies. if you’re staying on it, [v0.4.2](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/gary-localhost-installer-mac/releases/tag/v0.4.2) keeps local jerry working with the new plugin.
