the [patch news page](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/the-patch-next) now waits for fresh news when its saved copy expires, so the first visitor after a new post shouldn’t need to reload to see it. previously, that visit could show yesterday’s news while the site fetched the update in the background. the [fix](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/the-patch-next/pull/18) checks again after one minute and keeps the last valid feed if an update fails. we tested new posts, expired copies and invalid feeds before [deploying it](https://github.com/betweentwomidnights/the-patch-next/actions/runs/45849304513).
